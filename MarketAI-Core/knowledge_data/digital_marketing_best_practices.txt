DIGITAL AND LOCAL MARKETING BEST PRACTICES FOR RETAIL & MSMES:

1. WhatsApp Business Marketing Guidelines:
- Message formatting: Use bold asterisks (*text*) for key offers and emojis (🎉, 🎁, 🛍️) to break visual clutter. Keep it under 150 words.
- Localized greetings: Use local Indian languages (Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, etc.) depending on the region to build trust.
- Clear Call to Action (CTA): Provide a direct action link (e.g., "Scan this QR code at our store to get 20% off"). Do not use multiple CTAs.
- Share timing: Best shared on Saturday mornings (10 AM - 12 PM) or Sunday evenings (6 PM - 8 PM) when families plan outings.

2. Instagram and Facebook Best Practices:
- Visuals: Use high-contrast colorful graphics showing the product in action. Indian audiences respond well to vibrant colors (reds, yellows, golds) and festival themes.
- Hashtag Strategy: Combine 3 levels of hashtags: 1 broad category (#Bakery, #Fashion), 2 local/geographic (#MangaloreFoodies, #BangaloreBakers), and 1 brand-specific (#MarketAILocal).
- Engagement: Add a question in the caption (e.g. "What is your favorite festival sweet?") to boost comment sections and feed ranking.

3. Google Business Profile (GBP) Local SEO:
- Updates: Publish at least one "Update/Offer" weekly containing the campaign keyword (e.g. "Diwali Special").
- Details: Ensure opening hours, address, and phone number match physical signage exactly (consistent NAP).
- Review Loops: Encourage customers to scan a QR code at checkout to leave a 5-star review, offering a small freebie or discount in return.

4. Offline flyer and pamphlet distributions:
- Target spots: Hand them out near colleges, shopping complexes, or include them inside morning newspapers.
- Structure: Clear hook at the top, a prominent visual, the core discount code, and a trackable QR code linking to the location.
