GOVERNMENT MSME SCHEMES FOR SMALL BUSINESS MARKETING IN INDIA:

1. Procurement and Marketing Support (PMS) Scheme:
- Objective: To promote new market initiatives, create awareness, and develop marketing links for MSMEs.
- Key benefits: Funding support for participating in domestic and international trade fairs, exhibitions, and expos. Up to 80% to 100% subsidy on stall rental charges for micro and small enterprises.
- Advertising subsidies: Financial assistance for organizing seminars, workshops, and awareness campaigns on digital marketing and modern retail techniques.

2. Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE):
- Objective: Provide collateral-free credit to micro and small businesses to scale their operations, including digital and offline marketing expansions.
- Loan limit: Up to ₹5 Crore collateral-free credit facilities.

3. Financial Support to MSMEs in ZED Certification Scheme:
- Objective: Encourage Zero Defect Zero Effect manufacturing.
- Key benefits: Certification subsidies up to 80% for micro, 60% for small, and 50% for medium enterprises. ZED-certified businesses get preferential pricing on e-commerce platforms and government tenders.

4. Digital MSME Scheme:
- Objective: Promote Information & Communication Technology (ICT) in MSME operations.
- Key benefits: Subsidy for cloud computing, online ERP software, web portals, digital brochures, and automated social media tools to reduce marketing overheads.
